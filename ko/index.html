신청자 중 추첨을 통해 Steam과 PlayStation 테스트 키를 메일로 보내드리는 점 참고해주시고, 위시리스트와 팔로우도 부탁 드려요!

Rather than staying inside the studio, we want to get in front of players early and build a better game together, so we've prepared this Closed Alpha Test. This test is your first step in taking part in WPCA's development.

The build you'll be playing is a very early alpha with only the bare bones in place. You may run into unexpected bugs and outages, most systems are unfinished, and a lot will change from here. There's plenty still missing, so we'd be grateful for your patience as we work toward a great game.

Steam and PlayStation test keys will be emailed to applicants selected by draw. And please wishlist and follow us!
